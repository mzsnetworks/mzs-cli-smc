# Facebook — sold-on-speed


Wi-Fi 7 will be sold on speed and bought for capacity, and most enterprises will get neither.

The headline numbers are real in the sense that a lab can produce them. They're also the ceiling, and almost nobody is near their ceiling.

The constraint in a typical office is the uplink from the access point — often still a gigabit port into a switch from the last refresh. A faster radio behind a saturated uplink produces a faster queue.

The second is placement. Wider channels make placement matter more, not less, because channel reuse gets harder as channels get wider.

Buy Wi-Fi 7 when the thing limiting you is the radio.
