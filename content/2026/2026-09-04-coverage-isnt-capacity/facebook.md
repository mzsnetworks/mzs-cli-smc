# Facebook — coverage-isnt-capacity

Coverage isn't capacity.

The survey comes back green in every corner. The heat map goes in the report. And the complaints keep arriving — because users don't feel dBm. They feel contention. 📶

A radio is a shared medium — one device talks at a time, everyone else waits. Forty devices on one AP aren't forty conversations; they're one conversation, taken in turns.

Which is why the slowest client sets the tax everyone pays. A device at the edge, negotiating a low data rate, holds the medium longer to move the same data — slow for its owner, slow for everyone on that radio.

Adding power usually makes it worse. Bigger cells mean more clients per radio. The heat map gets greener while the experience degrades.

A heat map tells you the signal arrived. It doesn't tell you the user got a turn to speak.
