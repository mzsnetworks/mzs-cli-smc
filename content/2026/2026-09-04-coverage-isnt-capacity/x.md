## Thread (alternate)

1/ Coverage isn't capacity. The survey comes back green in every corner, and the complaints keep arriving. 🧵

2/ Users don't feel dBm. They feel contention.

3/ A radio is a shared medium. One device talks at a time, everyone else waits. Forty devices on one AP aren't forty conversations — they're one conversation, taken in turns, very quickly.

4/ Which is why the slowest client sets the tax everyone pays. A device at the edge negotiating a low data rate holds the medium longer to move the same data. Slow for its owner, slow for everyone on that radio.

5/ None of that is on a coverage map. The map answers "did signal reach here." The complaint is "did I get a turn."

6/ First numbers to look at:
• channel utilization
• retry rate
• clients per radio at peak
• the data rates in use

7/ And adding power usually makes it worse. Bigger cells, more clients per radio, more overlap. The heat map gets greener while the experience degrades.

8/ A heat map tells you the signal arrived. It doesn't tell you the user got a turn to speak. #WiFi #NetOps
