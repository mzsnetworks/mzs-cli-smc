# Facebook — knowing-what-matters-here


AI is good at the part we're bad at, and bad at the part we're good at.

It's good at reading everything. Every log line, every config, every ticket from the last three years, without getting bored on page four hundred.

It's bad at knowing what matters here. In this estate, on this night.

A model will tell you an interface reset forty times last month. It won't tell you that interface feeds a warehouse on a day shift, so the resets land at 2am and nobody noticed.

That context isn't written down. It lives in the heads of people who've been there a while.

AI reads your environment. It doesn't understand your business.

What does your team know that's written down nowhere?
