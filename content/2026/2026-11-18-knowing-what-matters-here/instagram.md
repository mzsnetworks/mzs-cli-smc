# Instagram — knowing-what-matters-here


AI is good at the part we're bad at. 🤖 And bad at the part we're good at.

Good at: reading everything. Every log, every config, every ticket from three years, without getting bored on page four hundred.

Bad at: knowing what matters here. In this estate, on this night. ⚠️

A model will tell you an interface reset forty times last month.

It won't tell you that interface feeds a day-shift warehouse — so the resets land at 2am and nobody noticed.

It'll flag drift on a device nobody has rebooted since 2021 — not that this is the thing to fix. ⚙️

That context isn't written down. It lives in the heads of people who've been there a while.

The teams getting value aren't the ones with the best tool. They're the ones with enough written down to point it at something real.

What does your team know that's written down nowhere?
