# Instagram — escalate-early-escalate-clean

Escalate early, escalate clean. 🔥

I used to hold burning issues way too long — escalating felt like admitting I couldn't do my job. The outage clock doesn't care about pride.

Junior move: hold it to prove you can solve it alone.
Senior move: a handoff so clean the next person starts where you stopped.

The evidence packet, three lines:
→ what changed — last known-good, first known-bad, everything between
→ what you ruled out — with the outputs that prove it
→ what you need — a decision, an access, a vendor case. Name it.

Timing rule: escalate when your learning rate drops. Re-running the same commands hoping for new output isn't troubleshooting — it's coping. ⏱️

Held issues age into outages. Shared issues age into fixes.

What's your escalation trigger — a timer, or a gut feeling? 👇
