# Facebook — root-cause-is-a-decision


Root cause is usually a decision, not a device.

Five whys works right up until the honest answer names a tradeoff somebody made. Then it quietly stops.

The supervisor failed. Why did that take the site down? The redundant path wasn't carrying routes. Why? A migration config was never reverted. Why wasn't it caught? Validation was dropped when the window got compressed.

That's where most reports end, and the finding reads "hardware failure."

But the chain was still going. The window got compressed because a business deadline was fixed. Somebody weighed a real risk against a real cost and chose — probably correctly.

Naming that isn't blame. It's the only way to change the outcome, because the device was never the variable.

Where do your five whys usually stop?
