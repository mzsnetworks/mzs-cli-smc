# Instagram — root-cause-is-a-decision


Root cause is usually a decision, not a device. ⚖️

Five whys works right up until the honest answer names a tradeoff somebody made. Then it quietly stops.

→ The supervisor failed
→ The redundant path wasn't carrying routes
→ A migration config was never reverted
→ Validation was dropped when the window got compressed
→ Why was it compressed?

📄 That's where the report ends. The finding reads "hardware failure."

But the chain was still going — a fixed business deadline, a real risk weighed against a real cost, a choice that was probably right.

Naming it isn't blame. The device was never the variable. 🔧

Where do your five whys usually stop?

## Carousel slide ideas

1. **Cover** — "Root cause is usually a *decision*, not a device."
2. **Where it stops** — Five whys works until the honest answer names a tradeoff.
3. **The chain** — The supervisor failed. The redundant path wasn't carrying routes.
4. **Deeper** — A migration config was never reverted. Validation was dropped.
5. **The last why** — "Why was the window compressed?" — and the document ends.
6. **The finding** — "Hardware failure."
7. **What was actually true** — A fixed deadline. A real risk against a real cost. A choice that was probably right.
8. **Not blame** — The device was never the variable. You can't buy a supervisor that survives silent loss of redundancy.
9. **Two habits** — Record the constraint, not just the event. Separate the decision from the outcome.
10. **CTA** — "A postmortem that ends at a component found *where* it surfaced — not why it was possible."
