# Facebook — two-sets-of-assumptions


An acquisition doesn't join two networks. It joins two sets of assumptions about who is trusted — and nobody documented either.

The technical work gets scoped first because you can see it: overlapping address space, routing between two cores, DNS both ways.

That is the easy half. The half that overruns is what nobody wrote down.

Somewhere in the acquired estate is a flat segment carrying production because everyone sat in one building ten years ago. Somewhere in yours is a firewall rule for a project that ended in 2021.

Neither is written down as a policy. Both are policies.

Connect them and you inherit the weaker of the two, applied to everything.

Which assumptions would you be inheriting?
