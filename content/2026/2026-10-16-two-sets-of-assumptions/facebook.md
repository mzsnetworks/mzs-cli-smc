# Facebook — two-sets-of-assumptions


An acquisition doesn't join two networks. It joins two sets of assumptions about who is trusted — and nobody documented either one.

The technical work gets scoped first because it's the part you can see: overlapping address space, routing between two cores, DNS resolving both ways. That's real work, and it's the easy half.

The half that overruns is the part nobody wrote down.

Somewhere in the acquired estate is a flat segment carrying production traffic because ten years ago everyone was in one building. Somewhere in yours is a rule permitting a range that made sense for a project that ended in 2021.

Neither is written down as a policy. Both are policies.

Connect them and you get the weaker of the two postures, applied to everything.

Which assumptions would you be inheriting?
