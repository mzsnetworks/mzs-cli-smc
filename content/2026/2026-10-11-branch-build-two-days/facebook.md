# Facebook — branch-build-two-days


A branch build should take two days. In most organizations it takes two weeks.

The gap is rarely engineering time. It's waiting.

Someone requests addressing and waits. Someone asks which switch is standard this quarter and gets two answers. The config gets copied from the last site, typo included.

None of that is difficult. All of it is undecided.

Four things collapse it: a parameterized template, addressing allocated from a system, a staging step before equipment ships, a written acceptance test.

What makes it work isn't the automation. It's that four decisions get made once instead of being renegotiated at every site.

How long does your fortieth site take compared to your fourth?
