# Facebook — branch-build-two-days


A branch build should take two days. In most organizations it takes two weeks.

The gap is almost never engineering time. It's waiting.

Someone requests addressing and waits. Someone asks which switch is standard this quarter and gets two answers. The config gets copied from the last site, carrying a typo that has propagated through four buildings.

None of that is difficult. All of it is undecided.

Four things collapse it, none of which cost anything: one parameterized template, one addressing scheme allocated from a system, one staging step before equipment ships, one written acceptance test.

What makes it work isn't the automation. It's that four decisions get made once instead of being renegotiated at every site.

How long does your fortieth site take compared to your fourth?
