# Instagram — branch-build-two-days


A branch build should take two days. Most take two weeks. 🏗️

The gap is almost never engineering time. It's waiting.

→ Addressing requested, then waiting for a reply
→ "Which switch is standard this quarter?" — two answers
→ Config copied from the last site, typo and all
→ Circuit arrives, nobody owns the test

None of that is difficult. All of it is undecided.

Four fixes, none of which cost anything:
⚙️ One parameterized template
📋 One addressing scheme, allocated from a system
📦 One staging step before it ships
✅ One written acceptance test

The second branch is where you find out whether you built a template or just another config.

How long does your fortieth site take vs your fourth?

## Carousel slide ideas

1. **Cover** — "A branch build should take two days. Most take *two weeks*."
2. **The gap isn't engineering** — It's waiting.
3. **Where the two weeks go** — Addressing requested and waited on. Two answers about the standard switch. A config copied from a copy.
4. **The typo that travels** — Carried through four buildings, because nobody wrote the template.
5. **None of it is difficult** — All of it is undecided.
6. **One template** — Parameterized. Fill in values, don't copy and edit.
7. **One addressing scheme** — Allocated from a system, not a spreadsheet.
8. **One staging step** — Configured and validated before it ships.
9. **One acceptance test** — Including one that proves traffic takes the path you intended.
10. **CTA** — "Four decisions made *once* — not renegotiated at every site."
