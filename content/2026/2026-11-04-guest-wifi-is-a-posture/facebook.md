# Facebook — guest-wifi-is-a-posture


Guest Wi-Fi is a security posture, not an amenity.

It gets designed around the splash page — the logo, the terms checkbox, whether it collects an email. That's the least interesting decision in it, and usually the only one reviewed.

The ones that matter:

What can it reach? The internet plus three things somebody needed once.
What can it resolve? A guest VLAN on the internal resolver enumerates the estate from the parking lot.
How long does a device stay on it? A lease measured in days isn't a guest network.
When it gets abused, can you name the device?

The portal is a greeting. Design the parts that carry risk.

What can your guest network reach that you wouldn't put in writing?
