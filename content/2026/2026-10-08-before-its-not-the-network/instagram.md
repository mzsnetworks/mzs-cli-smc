# Instagram — before-its-not-the-network


"It's not the network" costs you credibility if you're wrong once. 🎯

Be wrong once and it follows you. Every incident after, somebody quietly checks behind you — and the sentence stops carrying weight exactly when you need it to.

Six things I check before saying it:

1️⃣ Can I reproduce it from somewhere else? One client and every client are different incidents.

2️⃣ What do the counters say at both ends? Not "up" — errors, discards, light levels, and whether any of it is moving.

3️⃣ Does it behave the same in both directions? Asymmetry makes a one-way problem look like everyone's.

4️⃣ Where did the traffic actually go? What the client resolved and which path it took. Confirmed, not assumed.

5️⃣ Did anything scheduled run? Backups, replication, a batch window. Load on a timetable looks like a network fault to anyone without the timetable.

6️⃣ Is the application healthy on its own terms? Can it reach its database. Is it out of connections.

None of it is exotic. None of it takes long. What it buys is the right to say the sentence.

The useful version is never "it's not the network" alone. It's "it's not the network, and here's where it actually is." ⚙️

The first ends a conversation. The second ends an outage.
