# Facebook — cheapest-bid-loses-decade

The cheapest bid usually wins the project and loses the decade. 💰

We're not arguing for expensive. We're arguing that the number on the comparison sheet is the wrong number — and everybody knows it by year three.

What a low bid buys: fewer discovery hours, so surprises arrive later as change orders. No documentation line. No knowledge transfer — the environment leaves with the people who built it.

The costs land later, on a different budget, usually on the operations team who never saw the bid.

Two questions separate bids fast: what did you assume that, if wrong, becomes a change order? And who specifically is doing this work?

We lose bids on price, and this isn't a complaint. The fix is for the request to ask for the figure that includes the decade.

You don't buy a network once. You buy it every year for ten years.
