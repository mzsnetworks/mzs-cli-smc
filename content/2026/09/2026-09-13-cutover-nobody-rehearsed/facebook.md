# Facebook — cutover-nobody-rehearsed

Every project we've been called into late failed at the same milestone.

Not design. Not staging — the gear arrives, gets built, configured, and works on the bench.

It fails at the cutover — the first time any of it runs together. ⚠️

Everything before was tested in pieces. The cutover is the only integration test the project gets — and it runs in production, once, at 2am.

Reading the runbook aloud in a conference room isn't a rehearsal. A rehearsal has the actual commands, the actual person, the actual order, and a clock.

What that surfaces: the ten-minute step that takes forty, the order dependency nobody wrote down, and the back-out that's only ever been written.

Design gets reviewed. Hardware gets staged. The sequence gets discovered — at 2am, in front of everyone.

When did you last rehearse a back-out?
