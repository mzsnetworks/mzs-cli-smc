# Facebook — supportable-at-3am

Five things make a network supportable at 3am. None of them are products. 🌙

1️⃣ Consistent naming — at 3am you type the name before you think. bld2-idf3-sw01 tells you which building to walk to. switch-new-2 tells you nothing.

2️⃣ One source of truth — when two systems disagree about a VLAN, which wins? If it's "check with Dave," Dave is your source of truth. And Dave is asleep.

3️⃣ Out-of-band that actually works — can you reach a console while the uplink is down, without transiting the thing that's broken?

4️⃣ Backups someone has restored — a green checkmark is an unverified claim. The backup is rarely the problem. The restore is.

5️⃣ One named owner per system — "Escalate to Network" is a queue, not an owner.

At 3am nobody is clever. You only have what you wrote down while it was daylight.

Which one would you fail tonight?
