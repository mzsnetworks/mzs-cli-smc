# Instagram — outsourcing-isnt-accountability

You can outsource the work. You can't outsource the consequence.

There are good reasons to bring in a partner. ⚙️

→ after-hours coverage
→ specialist depth
→ a queue somebody else staffs at 3am
→ hands you can't currently justify hiring

We do this work. It's real value.

But something doesn't move with the contract.

When the site is down, the person the business turns to still works for the business.

The provider owns a scope. You own the outcome.

Which makes outsourcing the wrong question. The question is what you deliberately keep. 🔒

📍 One named owner
Somebody internal who can say yes or no to risk without calling a meeting. A provider can recommend an action at 2am. Only you can accept the consequence of it.

📍 Architecture direction
Rent the hands. Rent the design help. But the shape of the environment follows the business — and only you know where the business is going next year.

📍 Enough literacy to argue
If nobody on your side can challenge the report you're handed, you didn't buy a service. You bought a story.

📍 A clean exit
Where do the configs live? Who holds the credentials? Is the tooling yours or theirs? You want those answers on a normal Tuesday, not during a divorce.

The uncomfortable version, and we'll say it about ourselves: the best partnerships we're in are the ones where the client can argue with us.

You can hand over the runbook. You can hand over the pager. You can't hand over the risk.

If your provider went dark Monday, how long before you knew what to do? 👇
