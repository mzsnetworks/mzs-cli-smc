# Facebook — outsourcing-isnt-accountability

You can outsource the work. You can't outsource the consequence.

When the site is down, the person the business turns to still works for the business. The provider owns a scope. You own the outcome. 🔒

So the question isn't what you hand over. It's what you keep:

🔹 One named owner who can accept risk without calling a meeting
🔹 Architecture direction — only you know where the business is going next year
🔹 Enough literacy to argue — if nobody internally can challenge the report, you bought a story
🔹 A clean exit — where configs live, who holds credentials

The best partnerships we're in are the ones where the client can argue with us.

You can hand over the runbook. You can hand over the pager. You can't hand over the risk.

If your provider went dark on Monday, how long before you knew what to do?
