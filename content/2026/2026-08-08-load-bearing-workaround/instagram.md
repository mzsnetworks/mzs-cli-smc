6 signs yours became load-bearing infrastructure — swipe 👉

→ it has a nickname
→ new hires get taught it
→ its alert is permanently suppressed
→ something downstream depends on its quirks
→ nobody remembers the problem it solved

Workarounds become load-bearing through time, not approval. Every day nobody removes it is a sign-off nobody signed.

Two honest options: promote it like real infrastructure, or give its removal a date. Calling it temporary isn't one of them.
