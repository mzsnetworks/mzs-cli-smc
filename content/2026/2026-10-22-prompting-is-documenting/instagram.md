# Instagram — prompting-is-documenting


Prompting is the new documenting. 📝

More literally than it sounds.

Watch two engineers use the same model on the same task. One gets something usable. The other gets something plausible and wrong, and decides the tool is overrated.

The difference is almost never the phrasing. It's that the first one stated the constraints:

→ Which platform, which version
→ What the device already has on it
→ What must not change
→ What "correct" looks like when it's finished

Those aren't prompt-engineering tricks. That's what a competent change record has always required — and what was missing from every wiki page any of us has cursed at. ⚠️

Here's the uncomfortable part. The model isn't rewarding a new skill. It's exposing an old one, immediately and in public.

The gap between what you meant and what you said now comes back as output in four seconds, instead of as an outage in four months.

We used to hide imprecision. A vague runbook still worked, because a human silently supplied the missing half from context. Models don't supply context you didn't give them — they supply something that looks like it. ⚙️

When you can't get a useful answer out of a model, usually you haven't decided something.

Has a model ever told you that?
