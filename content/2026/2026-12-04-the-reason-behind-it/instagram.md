# Instagram — the-reason-behind-it


Your most valuable asset isn't the configuration. 🧠 It's the reason behind it.

The config is recoverable — backup, device, repository.

What isn't recoverable:

→ Why the timers on that one link are different from every other link
→ Why a route-map excludes a prefix that looks like it belongs
→ Why one site runs older firmware, and has for three years

Every one has a reason, arrived at during an outage nobody wants to repeat. ⚠️

The failure mode isn't that the person leaves. Somebody tidies up: a new engineer corrects an inconsistency because consistency is good practice — and reintroduces a problem solved years ago.

You don't prevent that with access control. You make the reason travel with the configuration. ⚙️

What in your network only one person understands?
