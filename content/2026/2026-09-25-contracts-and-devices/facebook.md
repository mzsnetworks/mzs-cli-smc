# Facebook — contracts-and-devices

We counted forty-two support contracts and thirty-one devices. 📋

Nobody had done anything wrong. No fraud, no incompetent purchasing — six years of ordinary decisions, and nothing that ever forced the two lists to meet.

A device is replaced and the new one gets a new contract, while the old one quietly renews. A spare is bought with coverage and sits in a cupboard. A site closes, but the support line was in a different budget owned by a different person.

Procurement tracks contracts. Operations tracks devices. Nothing reconciles them.

And the direction everyone assumes is only half of it. Some contracts covered hardware that no longer existed — but the reconciliation also found production devices with no coverage at all.

Overpaying is a line item. Finding out at 2am that your core switch has no RMA path is an outage with no known length.

When did anyone last compare what you pay for against what you actually run?
