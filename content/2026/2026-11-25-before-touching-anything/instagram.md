# Instagram — before-touching-anything


Taking over an environment you didn't build? 📋 The first week is collection, not change.

Five things, before anything gets touched:

→ Configs from every device, pulled the same day — not the backup repo, which is a month stale
→ The change record for twelve months. Read it for the gaps as much as the entries
→ Monitoring configuration, not dashboards. What's watched — and what isn't
→ Whatever automation exists, including the parts nobody runs. It says what somebody already tried
→ The list of things people are afraid to touch ⚠️

That last one isn't a document. You get it by asking, and the answers are specific: that switch, that rule, that site.

Every item marks a real event somebody lived through. ⚙️

None of this changes anything — which is the point. It's the only week naive questions are free.
