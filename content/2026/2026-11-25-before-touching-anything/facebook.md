# Facebook — before-touching-anything


When we take over an environment we didn't build, the first week is collection, not change.

Five things, before anything gets touched:

Configs from every device, pulled the same day — not the backup repo, which is usually stale.
Twelve months of change records, read for the gaps as much as the entries.
Monitoring configuration, not dashboards. What's watched, and what isn't.
Whatever automation exists, including the parts nobody runs.
The list of things people are afraid to touch. You get that one by asking.

The first week is the only week you can ask naive questions without cost.

What would somebody find in your estate that nobody warned them about?
