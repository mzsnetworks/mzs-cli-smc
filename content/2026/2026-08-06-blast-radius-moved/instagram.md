# Instagram — blast-radius-moved

Automation didn't reduce your outages. It changed who causes them. 💥

The blast radius moved from a human to a playbook.

Before: one fat-fingered VLAN, one closet down. Contained.
After: one wrong decision, encoded once, pushed everywhere. In seconds. Perfectly.

Automation executes decisions — it doesn't improve them.

The discipline has to move upstream:
→ dry-run diff before push
→ canary scope before fleet
→ rollback that's tested, not assumed
→ peer review on playbooks — they ARE production code

Automation doesn't take humans out of the loop. It amplifies whichever human wrote the loop. 🔁

What guardrail won't you let a playbook run without? 👇
