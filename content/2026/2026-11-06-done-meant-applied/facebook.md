# Facebook — done-meant-applied


A maintenance window that finishes two hours early is not good news.

We ask what got skipped. Usually nothing was, not on purpose. The work went in cleanly, nothing threw an error, the team was tired, and "applied without error" quietly became "done."

Those aren't the same state, and the gap between them is where Monday morning lives.

Every command returns clean. Interfaces up, device reachable, config matches what was staged. By every signal available at 2am, it worked. Then real traffic arrives.

The window wasn't too short. The definition of done was too small.

What does "done" mean in your change process?
