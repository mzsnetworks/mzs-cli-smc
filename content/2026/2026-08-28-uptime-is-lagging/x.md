## Thread (alternate)

1/ Uptime is a lagging indicator. It tells you what already survived — and nothing about what's about to break. 🧵

2/ We report it because it's the easiest number in the building to count. Four nines looks like a job well done, right up until the quarter it isn't.

3/ The pair worth watching instead: how fast the environment changes, measured against how much review those changes actually get.

4/ Velocity itself isn't the risk. Per DORA's State of DevOps research, the fastest teams are also the most stable. Throughput and stability rise together — they aren't a trade.

5/ The risk is velocity outrunning review discipline. That gap is visible long before an outage.

6/ • the "standard change" list keeps growing
• approvals come back in under a minute — approvals, not readings
• rollback is a paragraph nobody has executed
• the person who understands the change approves it

7/ Every one of those is measurable this week. None of them need an incident first.

8/ Uptime tells you what survived. Change velocity tells you what's coming. Review discipline decides which one you get. #NetOps #NetDevOps
