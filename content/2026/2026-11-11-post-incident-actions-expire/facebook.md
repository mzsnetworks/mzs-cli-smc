# Facebook — post-incident-actions-expire


Post-incident actions expire, and almost nobody notices.

The findings are good. The timeline is honest. At the bottom there's a list of actions. Come back in six months: one got done, because somebody was annoyed enough that week. The rest aged out quietly.

That isn't laziness. The list was written at the one moment nobody could say no to it — when agreement cost nothing, because the work had no date, owner or budget attached.

Two weeks later the regret faded and the work still has none of those. It competes with the roadmap on equal terms, and loses.

No name, no date, no place in somebody's workload? Not an action.

How many actions from your last postmortem have a date on them?
