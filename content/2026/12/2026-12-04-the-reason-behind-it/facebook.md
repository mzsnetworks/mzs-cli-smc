# Facebook — the-reason-behind-it


Your most valuable asset isn't the configuration. It's the reason behind it — and it usually lives in one person's head.

The config is recoverable. It's in a backup, on the device, in a repository.

What isn't recoverable is why the timers on that one link are different. Why a route-map excludes a prefix that looks like it belongs. Why one site runs older firmware and has for three years.

The failure mode isn't that the person leaves. Somebody tidies up — a new engineer corrects an inconsistency because consistency is good practice, and reintroduces a problem solved years ago.

Make the reason travel with the configuration.

What in your network only one person understands?
