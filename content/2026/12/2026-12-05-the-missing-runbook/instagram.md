# Instagram — the-missing-runbook


Every AIOps tool promises to cut alert noise. 🔕

The noise was never the problem. The missing runbook was.

The pitch isn't dishonest. Thousands of events become dozens of incidents. The thing that fired four hundred times becomes one line. The tools work.

What I've also seen is a team six months later, ignoring far fewer alerts. ⚠️

An alert isn't noise because there are many of it. It's noise because nobody knows what to do when it arrives.

→ One alert a week that nobody can act on is noise
→ Two hundred a day that each have a known response are a busy Tuesday

The question that sorts them: when this fires at 3am, what is the person supposed to do?

If the answer is "look at it and decide," that isn't an alert. It's an interruption with a technical description attached. ⚙️

The honest sequence is the opposite of the sales one. Sort what fires into three piles:

→ Clear response — fine
→ Needs a response written — the actual work, and it takes weeks
→ Shouldn't exist — usually the largest pile, and the fastest win nobody takes

Then correlate. On top of alerts that mean something, correlation is valuable. On top of alerts nobody can act on, it produces a tidier version of the same problem.

A tool tells you what happened. Not what you do about it.

How many of your alerts have a documented response?
