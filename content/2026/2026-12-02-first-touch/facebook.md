# Facebook — first-touch


The quality of a support case is the one variable you fully control.

Open with "the VPN is slow for some users" and you've started a four-day conversation — because the first two exchanges are them asking what you could have said at the start.

Five things, on the first touch:

Topology — the specific path, including the proxy added last year.
Timestamps with a timezone. The engineer reading this is somewhere else.
Captures from both sides, taken at the same time.
What changed — including "nothing we know of, here's the record anyway."
What you already ruled out, and how.

A case is a document you're asking a stranger to act on.
