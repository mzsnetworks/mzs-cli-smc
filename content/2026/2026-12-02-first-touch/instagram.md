# Instagram — first-touch


The quality of a support case is the one variable you fully control. 🎫

Open with "the VPN is slow for some users" and you've started a four-day conversation — the first two exchanges are them asking what you could have said at the start. ⚠️

Five things, on the first touch:

→ Topology. Not the corporate diagram. The specific path, including the proxy added last year
→ Timestamps with a timezone. The engineer reading this is somewhere else
→ Captures from both sides, taken at the same moment
→ What changed — including "nothing we know of, here's the change record anyway"
→ What you already ruled out, and how

That last one moves a case out of tier one — the reader doesn't start from the beginning. ⚙️

A case is a document you're asking a stranger to act on. Write it like one.
