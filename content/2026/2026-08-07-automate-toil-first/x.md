## Thread (alternative)

1/ The 5 tasks worth automating first are never the ones people ask for. 🧵

2/ People ask for the demo: a self-service portal, a chatbot, auto-remediation. Impressive on a projector. Irrelevant at 3am.

3/ What pays:
• state capture before/after changes
• config backup + drift alerts
• pre/post-maintenance health checks
• the recurring restart runbook
• alert enrichment

4/ Notice: none of these change the network. They capture, verify, inform. Automation earns trust by being right before it earns write access.

5/ The demo automates what impresses. The discipline automates what pages you. What pages your team most? #NetworkAutomation #NetOps
