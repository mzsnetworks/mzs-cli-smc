# Instagram — four-incidents-taught-me


Four incidents taught me more than any certification I've sat. 🌙

It took me years to work out why.

A certification teaches you what's true. An incident teaches you what you do when you're not sure — which turns out to be most of the job.

1️⃣ Write down the time. I chased a fault all afternoon that had started before the change everyone blamed. Nobody could prove it either way, because nobody recorded when it began.

2️⃣ "We restarted it and it came back" is not a conclusion. It closed. It reopened four days later, and cost far more the second time.

3️⃣ Check the thing you're certain about. The certainty was the reason I hadn't checked it. It was wrong, and three hours went somewhere else. The check would have taken ninety seconds. ⚠️

4️⃣ The handover is the risk. A clean fix, undone at 6am because the incoming engineer didn't know a temporary change was still in place. Nobody made a mistake — the information just didn't travel.

None of them changed a fact I knew. Each changed a default I run on.

Facts you can look up in the moment. Defaults are what you have when there's no moment to look anything up. ⚙️
