# Instagram — five-questions-before-wireless


Every wireless RFP opens with an AP count. 📡

That number is an answer. Nobody asked the questions yet.

Five we ask before quoting:

→ How many devices in the worst room at the worst moment — not the average
→ What the walls are made of (concrete ≠ plasterboard ≠ foil-backed insulation)
→ What's actually connecting: the scanners, carts, badge readers nobody counted
→ What the application is — a voice call and an email want different designs
→ Who owns the ceiling 🪜

That last one moves dates. We've seen an RF design finish in two weeks and wait four months for ceiling access.

A quote that skips them isn't cheaper. It's the same project with the surprises still in it.

The AP count is the last thing we work out. It falls out of the answers.

What did your last wireless quote actually ask you?
