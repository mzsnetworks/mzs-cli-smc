# Instagram — rip-and-replace-fantasy

"Rip and replace" is a fantasy budget line. 🏗️

The slide: decommission old, stand up new, cut over on a weekend.
We've read that slide many times. We've never watched it happen.

The old network is carrying production right now:
→ sessions nobody can drop
→ an app nobody dares restart
→ a payment flow running through the "legacy" switch
→ dependencies documented only by breaking

Real modernization happens live. Run new beside old. Move one thing. Verify. Move the next. The old gear leaves last, not first. 🔁

Small, verified, reversible — reversible is the whole game in brownfield.

If the plan needs downtime nobody will approve, it isn't a plan. It's a wish.

How many rip-and-replace projects finish on the original slide? 👇
