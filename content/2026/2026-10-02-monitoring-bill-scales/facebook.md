# Facebook — monitoring-bill-scales

Your monitoring bill scales with data. Your understanding doesn't. 📊

Every device added, every metric enabled, every month of retention extended costs more. None of it automatically tells you anything new.

Most of that growth was never a decision. Default polling applied to thousands of interfaces because the template did it. Retention set at the maximum on day one, in case. Debug logging switched on during an investigation in 2023 and never switched off.

The uncomfortable test: take any metric you pay to store and ask when it last changed a decision. Not when it was last displayed — when it changed what somebody did.

Mostly, nobody can answer.

And the second-order cost is worse than the invoice. More data means more places to look, so it takes longer to find the one thing that mattered.

Collecting more is easy. Deciding what not to collect is the engineering.
