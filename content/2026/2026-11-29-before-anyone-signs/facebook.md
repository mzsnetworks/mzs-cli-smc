# Facebook — before-anyone-signs


"It pings" is not an acceptance test. It's a pulse check on a patient nobody has examined.

Five things separate a document that closes a project from one that protects you after it closes:

The application, not the link — run by somebody who'd recognize a wrong answer.
Both directions, separately. Upload and download fail differently.
The failure case, deliberately triggered. Not whether the route converges — whether the transaction completes.
Real load, at the hour that matters.
A named person who signs. Not a team, not a mailbox.

Each one can fail. A criterion that can't fail isn't a criterion.
