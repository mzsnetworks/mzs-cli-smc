# Instagram — before-anyone-signs


"It pings" is not an acceptance test. 📝 It's a pulse check on a patient nobody has examined.

Five things that make a test worth signing:

→ The application, not the link. Not that the circuit is up — that the thing it exists for completes, run by somebody who'd spot a wrong answer
→ Both directions, separately. They fail differently. Cheapest check on the list, most often skipped
→ The failure case, deliberately triggered. Not whether the route converges — whether the transaction completes
→ Behavior under real load, at the hour that matters. A test at 7am measures an empty network
→ A named person who signs. Not a team, not a mailbox ⚠️

What these share: each one can fail.

A criterion that can't fail isn't a criterion. Most acceptance tests we read are made entirely of those. ⚙️
