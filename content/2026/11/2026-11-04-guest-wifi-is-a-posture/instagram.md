# Instagram — guest-wifi-is-a-posture


Guest Wi-Fi is a security posture, not an amenity. 📶

It gets designed around the splash page. That's the least interesting decision in it ⚠️ and usually the only one reviewed.

The ones that matter are the ones nobody writes down:

→ What can it reach? The internet, plus a printer, a room display and a "temporary" file share
→ What can it resolve? A guest VLAN on the internal resolver enumerates the estate from the parking lot
→ How long does a device stay on it? A lease in days isn't a guest network
→ When it gets abused, can you name the device?

Not an argument for friction — a harder portal pushes people to a hotspot. ⚙️

Guest Wi-Fi is the one network you intend strangers to use. It should be the one you can describe most precisely.

What can your guest network reach that you wouldn't put in writing?
