# Instagram — without-asking-you


What would you actually let an AI change without asking you first? 🤖

Ask that in a room of engineers and the honest answer is almost always nothing. Not one interface. Not a route. Not a queue depth on a device serving eleven people. ⚠️

That answer is the real state of AIOps adoption — more useful than any maturity model.

Platforms are sold on detection, and detection has genuinely gotten good:

→ Thousands of events correlated into one probable cause
→ The pattern spotted hours before the pager
→ The specific action that fixes it, suggested

I've watched tools do all of that and be right.

Then a human reads the suggestion and types it in. Every time. So the value delivered is "noticed faster" — and the industry calls that autonomous operations.

The reluctance isn't about accuracy. ⚙️

Nobody says the recommendation is usually wrong. What they say is: I can't explain that change afterwards, and I can't get it back cleanly if it's wrong.

That reframes it. The blocker isn't model quality. It's that the surrounding system has no undo and no account of itself.

So the work is unglamorous, and it isn't AI work. Make changes reversible and legible.

Autonomy isn't something a platform grants you. It's something your rollback path earns.

What's the first thing you'd hand over, and what would have to be true first?
