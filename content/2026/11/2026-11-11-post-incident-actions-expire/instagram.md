# Instagram — post-incident-actions-expire


Post-incident actions expire. ⏳ Almost nobody notices.

The findings are good. The timeline is honest. At the bottom, a list of actions:

→ Add monitoring here
→ Document that
→ Review the other thing quarterly

Come back in six months. One got done — somebody was annoyed enough that week. The rest aged out quietly.

That isn't laziness. ⚠️

The list was written at the one moment nobody could say no to it. Agreement costs nothing when the work has no date, no owner, no budget yet.

Two weeks later the regret has faded. The work still has none of those, so it competes with the roadmap on equal terms and loses.

The test is blunt: no name, no date, no place in a real workload = not an action. ⚙️

It's a feeling somebody had on a bad day.

How many actions from your last postmortem have a date on them?
