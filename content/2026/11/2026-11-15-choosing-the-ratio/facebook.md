# Facebook — choosing-the-ratio


Oversubscription is not a dirty word. Undeclared oversubscription is.

Every network you've run was oversubscribed somewhere. The access layer assumes not every port transmits at once. That's what makes the network affordable.

The real problem isn't the ratio. It's that in most estates nobody can tell you what it is.

Somebody chose 20 to 1 in 2019, for reasons that made sense with 2019's traffic. Then video became constant and three sites started replicating. The ratio didn't change. The thing it was a bet about did.

A deliberate ratio that gets reviewed is engineering. An accidental one is a liability that happens to be working.

What is your access layer ratio, and who chose it?
