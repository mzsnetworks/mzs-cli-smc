# Instagram — choosing-the-ratio


Oversubscription isn't a dirty word. 📊 Undeclared oversubscription is.

Every network you've run is oversubscribed somewhere:

→ The access layer assumes not every port transmits at once
→ The uplink assumes not every switch bursts at once
→ The circuit assumes a fraction of what the LAN could generate

It's what makes the network affordable.

⚠️ The real problem isn't the ratio. It's that nobody can tell you what it is.

Somebody chose 20 to 1 in 2019, for reasons that made sense then. Then video became constant. A backup moved into business hours. Three sites started replicating.

The ratio didn't change. What it was a bet about did. ⚙️

A deliberate ratio that gets reviewed is engineering. An accidental one is a liability that happens to be working.

What is your access layer ratio, and who chose it?
