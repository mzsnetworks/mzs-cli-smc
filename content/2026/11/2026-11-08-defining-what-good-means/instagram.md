# Instagram — defining-what-good-means


SD-WAN didn't remove the WAN engineer. 🔀 It moved the hard part.

Configuring links used to be the work — two circuits, a routing protocol, a failover design on a whiteboard. That part is largely solved.

What replaced it is a question nobody wants to answer:

What does "good" mean for this application?

The policy engine enforces whatever you tell it. But it needs to know:

→ The ERP tolerates latency, not loss
→ Voice is the reverse
→ Replication takes what's left and competes with neither

⚠️ Those aren't network facts. They're business decisions about which application loses.

So it stalls in a meeting, not a lab. Every owner says "mine is critical" — the same as nobody answering. ⚙️

The classification is the deliverable. The overlay just enforces it.

Who in your organization is allowed to say which application loses?
