# Instagram — who-decides


Who owns the network? 🧭 Not who runs it — who decides.

Running it is easy. A team. A rota. An escalation path. Ask who fixes it at 2am and you get a name.

Deciding is harder:

→ Who approves a standard that constrains what a business unit can buy?
→ Who says the refresh happens this year, not next?
→ Who settles security vs. a shorter change window?

Those answers arrive as "it depends." ⚠️ Usually true. Never useful.

What we find: owned operationally by a team with no budget authority — and financially by somebody never asked a technical question about it.

Neither can decide alone. Nobody told them that. ⚙️

A network without an owner doesn't stop working. It stops changing — which looks the same for a while.

Who can approve a network standard without asking anyone?
