# Facebook — who-decides


Who owns the network in your organization? Not who runs it. Who decides.

Running it is easy to locate — a team, a rota, an escalation path. Ask who fixes it at 2am and you get a name.

Deciding is harder. Who approves a standard that constrains what a business unit can buy? Those answers arrive as "it depends."

What we usually find: the network is owned operationally by a team with no budget authority, and financially by somebody never asked a technical question about it. Neither can decide alone, and nobody told them that.

A network without an owner doesn't stop working. It stops changing, which looks the same for a while.

Who can approve a network standard without asking anyone?
