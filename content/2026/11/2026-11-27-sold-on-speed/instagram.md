# Instagram — sold-on-speed


Wi-Fi 7 will be sold on speed 📡 and bought for capacity. Most enterprises will get neither.

The headline numbers are real — in the sense that a lab can produce them. They're the ceiling, and almost nobody is near theirs. ⚠️

What's actually limiting you:

→ The uplink. Often a gigabit port into a switch from the last refresh — a faster radio behind a saturated uplink is a faster queue
→ Placement. APs go where there's cable and a convenient ceiling tile, rarely where the density is
→ The client mix. Benefits need both ends, and most device populations span eight years

Wider channels make placement matter more, not less. In a dense deployment the new standard can perform worse than the old one. ⚙️

The order is unglamorous: uplinks, then survey, then radios.
