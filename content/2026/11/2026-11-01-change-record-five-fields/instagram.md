# Instagram — change-record-five-fields


Most change records are written to be filed, not to be read. 📋

We ask for them on every takeover. Complete, and close to useless.

Ticket, requester, category, risk rating "low," approval chain, date. ⚠️ Everything an audit asks for. Nothing the person at 2am needs.

Five fields change that:

→ What changed — the actual diff, not "updated ACL per request"
→ Why — the condition that forced it, not the ticket that authorized it
→ Who approved it — a name. A queue is routing, not accountability
→ How to undo it — steps somebody has run once
→ What breaks, for whom, if this is wrong

That last one gets skipped. It tells the engineer at 2am whether to roll back or keep looking. ⚙️

A change record isn't a receipt. It's a message to whoever is awake when it matters.

Which of the five is missing from yours?
