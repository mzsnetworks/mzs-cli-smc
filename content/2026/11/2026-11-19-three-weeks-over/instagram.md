# Instagram — three-weeks-over


I missed a migration estimate by three weeks. 📉

Not because the work was harder than I thought. Because I priced the work, and the project was mostly discovery.

The plan was reasonable on paper:

→ A known number of sites
→ A known target design
→ A cutover procedure proven at the first two

Multiply, add contingency. That's how most brownfield projects get quoted. ⚠️

What consumed the three weeks wasn't cutovers. It was finding out what was installed.

Sites with a device not in the inventory. A link nobody could explain, carrying real traffic. A firewall rule referencing a host decommissioned years earlier — dead or load-bearing, and nobody could say which.

Each is a day or two, and none of it is cutover work. ⚙️

So I ask two questions before quoting anything brownfield now.

First: how was this inventory produced, and when. A discovery scan from last month is a different artifact from a spreadsheet kept by hand since 2019.

Second: when we find something undocumented, who decides. Not escalates — decides.

The schedule doesn't break on the discovery. It breaks on the four days it waits for somebody authorized to say "leave it."

A greenfield estimate prices the work. A brownfield estimate has to price the finding out.

What does your inventory actually say, and when was it last true?
