# Instagram — done-meant-applied


A maintenance window that finishes two hours early isn't good news. 🌙

We ask what got skipped. Usually nothing was — not on purpose.

The work went in cleanly. Nothing threw an error. The team was tired. And "applied without error" quietly became "done."

Those aren't the same state. ⚠️ The gap between them is where Monday morning lives.

The pattern is specific:

→ Change goes in Saturday night
→ Every command returns clean
→ Interfaces up, device reachable, config matches what was staged
→ By every signal available at 2am, it worked
→ Monday arrives with real traffic, and something that only shows under load fails

The window wasn't too short. The definition of done was too small.

"Applied" is a statement about the device. "Verified" is one about the service. ⚙️

What does "done" mean in your change process?
