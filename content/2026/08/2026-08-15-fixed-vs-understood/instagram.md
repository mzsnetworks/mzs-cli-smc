# Instagram — fixed-vs-understood

We "fixed" it by rebooting. The ticket closed. The problem didn't. 🔁

Reboot → metrics green → "resolved: rebooted, service restored."
Three weeks later: same symptom, same device, worse timing.

We hadn't fixed anything. We'd reset the evidence.

→ fixed = the symptom is gone right now
→ understood = you know why, and what brings it back
→ only one of them prevents the sequel

The reboot destroyed the crime scene — whatever was building toward failure lived in the state we cleared. Diagnosis traded for uptime.

Restoring first is the job. But it comes with a debt: capture before you clear, then split the ticket — service restored ✅, cause still open 🔍, capture armed for next time.

"Resolved: rebooted" isn't a resolution. It's a scheduled reopening.

Fixed closes tickets. Understood closes causes.

What issue in your network is on its third "fix"? 👇
