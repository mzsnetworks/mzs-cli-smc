# Facebook — uptime-is-lagging

Uptime is a lagging indicator. It tells you what survived — not what's about to break.

We report it because it's the easiest number to count. Four nines looks like a job well done — right up until the quarter it isn't.

Watch this pair instead: how fast the environment changes, measured against how much review those changes get.

Velocity itself isn't the risk. Per DORA's State of DevOps research, the fastest teams are also the most stable — throughput and stability rise together. The risk is velocity outrunning review.

The tells show up long before an outage. The "standard change" list keeps growing. Approvals come back in under a minute. Rollback is a paragraph nobody has executed.

Uptime tells you what survived. Change velocity tells you what's coming. ⚖️

When did anyone last check the ratio?
