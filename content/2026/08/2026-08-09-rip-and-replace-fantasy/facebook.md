# Facebook — rip-and-replace-fantasy

"Rip and replace" is a fantasy budget line.

The slide says: decommission the old network, stand up the new one, cut over on a weekend. We've read that slide many times. We've never watched it happen.

The old network is carrying production traffic right now — apps nobody dares restart, dependencies nobody documented. There's no weekend long enough for that.

Real modernization happens live: run new alongside old, move one thing, verify, move the next. Small, verified, reversible — the old gear leaves last, not first.

If the plan needs downtime nobody will approve, it isn't a plan. It's a wish.

How many rip-and-replace projects have you seen finish on the original slide?
