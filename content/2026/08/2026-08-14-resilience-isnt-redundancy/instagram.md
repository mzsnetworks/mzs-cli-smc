# Instagram — resilience-isnt-redundancy

Resilience isn't redundancy. Two of everything — and still down. ⚡

Dual supervisors, dual uplinks, dual ISPs. Unbreakable on paper. Then the primary fails and the failover fires for real — for the first time ever. In production.

What no invoice shows:
→ the standby that drifted for two years
→ "diverse" circuits in the same conduit
→ both power feeds on one panel
→ timers left at install defaults
→ nothing ever failed over on purpose

An untested failover isn't a safety net. It's an unknown. Your first test shouldn't be an outage with an audience. 🎯

The fix isn't more hardware. It's rehearsal — fail over on purpose, on a schedule, in a window.

Redundancy is what you buy. Resilience is what you rehearse.

When did your team last fail over on purpose? 👇
