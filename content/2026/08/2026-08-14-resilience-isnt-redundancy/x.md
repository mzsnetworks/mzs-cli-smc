1/ Resilience isn't redundancy. You can own two of everything and still go down. 🧵

2/ Dual supervisors, dual uplinks, dual ISPs. Unbreakable on paper — until the failover fires for real, for the first time ever, in production.

3/ What no invoice shows:
• a standby that drifted for years
• "diverse" circuits in one conduit
• both power feeds on one panel
• timers at install defaults

4/ An untested failover isn't a safety net. It's an unknown. Its failure modes are only revealed by failure itself.

5/ Fail over on purpose, on a schedule. Prove independence, not duplication. Redundancy you buy — resilience you rehearse. When did you last fail over on purpose? #NetOps #Resilience
