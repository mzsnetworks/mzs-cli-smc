# Instagram — switch-nobody-dared-reboot

Seven years of uptime. Nobody was proud of it. Everybody was afraid of it. ⏳

Every maintenance window: "not that one."

Installed by someone who left the company twice ago. Carrying VLANs that appeared on no diagram. Never once power-cycled on purpose.

What seven years of uptime actually tells you:

→ the recovery path has never been tested — config, licenses, optics that sometimes don't come back
→ every unpatched bug from seven years of advisories is still in there
→ the running config drifted from the saved config, and nobody can list how

That switch wasn't stable. It was untested. Two different states that look identical on a dashboard. 📉

And the fear compounds. Every year it stays up, rebooting gets scarier — the blast radius grows with everything you've hung off it since. Avoidance isn't free. It's a loan at a rising rate.

We finally did it. Saturday. Config backed up, console cable in hand, spare in the rack, vendor case already open.

Four minutes to boot. Two interfaces came back dirty, ten minutes to fix. 🔧

The disaster we feared for seven years took fourteen minutes — on a day we chose. The alternative was the same fourteen minutes on a day it chose.

You don't have a stable switch. You have an unscheduled outage with a countdown you can read.

What's the device everyone on your team quietly steers around? 👇
