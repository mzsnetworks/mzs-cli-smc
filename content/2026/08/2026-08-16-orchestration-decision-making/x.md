## Thread (alternate)

1/ Orchestration isn't automation at scale. It's decision-making at scale.

2/ The pitch: forty scripts, three teams, buy the orchestrator, now you have a platform.

3/ Then the real work shows up — questions nobody wrote down. What runs first. What blocks what. What happens when step 6 fails after step 5 already changed state.

4/ Scripts don't make decisions. The tool doesn't discover them either. It executes the dependency graph you hand it.

5/ Hand it a wrong graph and you've bought a faster way to be wrong across a hundred devices at once.

6/ The whiteboard test: can't draw the order, blockers and rollback path? Not ready to buy the tool that automates it.

7/ Scripts execute. Orchestrators decide. The decisions have to exist before a tool can hold them. #NetDevOps
