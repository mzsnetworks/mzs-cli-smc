# Facebook — nobody-told-us


Four of the worst outages we've been called into had nothing to do with the network.

Each started with a change somebody made without telling anyone who'd live with it.

A certificate update that a monitoring system had been pinning for three years. A patch field relabelled during a move — nothing electrical changed, but the labels were the only map. Compression enabled on a replication job, changing the traffic shape a threshold was tuned to.

Nobody was careless. Each change was reasonable inside the team that made it. The damage happened at the boundary.

Most change processes prove a change was authorized. Few make sure it was announced.

Who finds out when you change something?
