# Instagram — nobody-told-us


Four of the worst outages we've seen had nothing to do with the network. 🔌

Each started with a change nobody announced:

→ A certificate update — correct, tested, in remit — that a monitoring system had pinned for three years
→ A patch field relabelled during a move. Nothing electrical changed, but the labels were the only map
→ Compression on a replication job, changing the traffic shape a threshold was tuned to
→ A vendor firmware push through a support portal, no window

⚠️ Nobody was careless. Each change was reasonable inside the team that made it.

The damage happened at the boundary — where one team's correct decision met another's undocumented assumption. ⚙️

Most change processes prove authorization. Few ensure announcement.

Your last unexplained outage may have been somebody else's Tuesday.
