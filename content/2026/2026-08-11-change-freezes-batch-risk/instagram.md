# Instagram — change-freezes-batch-risk

Change freezes don't reduce risk. They batch it. 🧊

December: freeze everything. Feels safe. Reads well in a status meeting.
January: every postponed change lands in the same two weeks.

→ bigger diffs
→ stale context
→ everything moving at once
→ "what changed?" has thirty answers

Change risk = diff size × context staleness × how much else moves. A freeze inflates all three — then releases them together.

The honest version: freeze the risky and the optional. Keep the small and routine flowing. Staff the thaw like the event it is. 🔥

A freeze doesn't cancel risk. It schedules it.

Does your org freeze? What does the first week after look like? 👇
