## Thread (alternative)

1/ The 2am rollback isn't bad luck. It's a checklist that was never written. 🧵

2/ The four lines:
• pre-checks captured — state snapshotted first
• blast radius written down
• a back-out that's been RUN, not theorized
• one named decision-maker

3/ "Not sure what it can break" means the change isn't ready for a window — it's still in design.

4/ An untested rollback plan is a theory. Rehearse it, and mark the point of no return before you start.

5/ The checklist moves every decision out of the window. Plan at 2pm. Execute at 2am. Never think at 2am. #NetOps #ChangeManagement
