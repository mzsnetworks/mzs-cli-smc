# Facebook — diverse-carriers-shared-conduit


Two carriers. Two invoices. One conduit.

Before your next resilience review: which circuits have genuinely diverse paths? Not diverse carriers — diverse conduit.

Carrier diversity is commercial: two vendors, two bills. Path diversity is physical — where the fiber is actually buried. You can buy the first without getting the second.

Carriers lease capacity from each other, so both "redundant" links can leave the building on the same side and pass through the same manhole two miles away. Nobody lied — nobody had the whole picture.

You find out the day a backhoe finds that manhole and both circuits drop in the same second.

The fix isn't more redundancy. It's asking both carriers for the route. A refusal is itself information.

Have you asked a carrier where the fiber actually runs?
