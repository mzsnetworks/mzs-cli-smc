# Instagram — slow-is-not-a-symptom


"It's slow" is not a symptom. It's a feeling. 🐌

And you can't troubleshoot a feeling.

That's not a dig at whoever reported it — they're describing it accurately from where they sit. But the report has no number, no direction and no boundary.

Without those three, you can't tell whether anything you do next helps. ⚠️

The expensive version: a ticket open for a month, because there's no condition under which anyone could call it fixed.

Three things I get before touching anything:

→ A number. Slow compared to what, measured how. A transaction that took four seconds and now takes forty. Even a stopwatch. Forty seconds is a fact. "Slow" isn't.

→ A direction. One way, both ways, or only the first request? Upload and download failing differently is a completely different problem — and it's the cheapest split available.

→ A boundary. Who is NOT affected. One user, one site, one time of day. The edge tells you more than the center.

Then one that isn't technical: when did this become worth reporting? Sometimes nothing changed in the network and something changed in what the business expects of it. ⚙️

None of this is the investigation. It's what makes one possible.

An unmeasured complaint can't be closed. It can only be outlasted.

How do you get a number out of "it's slow"?
