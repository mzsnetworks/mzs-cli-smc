# Facebook — root-cause-is-a-decision


Root cause is usually a decision, not a device.

Five whys works until the honest answer names a tradeoff somebody made. Then it stops.

The supervisor failed. Why did that take the site down? The redundant path wasn't carrying routes — a migration config was never reverted, and validation was dropped when the window got compressed.

Most reports end there: "hardware failure."

The chain was still going. The window got compressed because a business deadline was fixed. Somebody weighed a real risk against a real cost and chose — probably correctly.

Naming that isn't blame. It's the only way to change the outcome — the device was never the variable.

Where do your five whys usually stop?
