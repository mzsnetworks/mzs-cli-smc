# Instagram — the-device-nobody-can-log-into


Every environment we inherit has one device nobody can log into. 🔒

It is never the least important one.

→ The core switch in the oldest building
→ The firewall in front of what finance uses on the 31st
→ The person who built it left in 2019

⚙️ And it works. Six years, no complaints — which is exactly why nobody ever opened it.

But this isn't a story about passwords. It's the point where operational ownership stopped transferring. Credentials are just the symptom you can see from outside.

So "get into the device" is the wrong goal. It's a ticket, not a fix.

That device isn't your risk. It's the only one that raised its hand. ✋

What would it take to find the other three?
