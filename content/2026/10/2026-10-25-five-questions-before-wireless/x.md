## Thread

1/ Every wireless RFP we receive opens with a number of access points.

That number is an answer. Nobody has asked the questions yet. 🧵

2/ How many devices in the worst room at the worst moment?

Not the average. The all-hands, the shift change, the exam hall.

Average density builds a network that fails on the days somebody is watching.

3/ What are the walls made of?

Plasterboard and glass behave nothing like poured concrete, foil-backed insulation, or a cold store.

An afternoon on site answers this. A floor plan does not.

4/ What is actually going to connect?

The laptops are easy. It's the decade-old barcode scanners that only speak 2.4 GHz, the medical carts, the badge readers.

One legacy client class can hold a whole band hostage.

5/ What is the application?

Email tolerates a roam that takes half a second. A voice call does not.

The application decides the layout, not the square footage.

6/ Who owns the ceiling?

Mounting, cable routes, conduit, asbestos surveys, landlord consent.

We've seen an RF design finish in two weeks and wait four months for ceiling access.

7/ None of these are difficult questions.

They're unwelcome at quoting time, because every one can change the number.

A quote that skips them isn't cheaper — it's the same project with the surprises still in it.
