# Facebook — five-questions-before-wireless


Every wireless RFP we receive opens with a number of access points.

That number is an answer. Nobody has asked the questions yet.

Five we ask before quoting, none of them AP count:

Density in the worst room at the worst moment, not the average. What the walls are made of — concrete and foil-backed insulation are not plasterboard. What is actually connecting, including the scanners that only speak 2.4 GHz. What the application is, because a voice call and an email want different designs. And who owns the ceiling, the question that moves dates.

A quote that skips them isn't cheaper. It's the same project with the surprises still in it.
