# Instagram — cloud-moved-the-failure-domain


The cloud didn't shrink your network. ☁️ It moved a failure domain somewhere you can't see.

Nobody designed your cloud connectivity. It accumulated, one urgent project at a time:

→ First workload moves, someone builds a VPN
→ Second gets its own
→ By the fourth: a direct connect, two tunnels nobody will remove, routing policy in three places that agrees in two

⚖️ On-prem, you owned every hop. Now the path runs through a provider edge you can't instrument.

The real cost isn't the outage. It's the hour spent proving whose outage it is. ⏱️

Where does your visibility actually stop?
