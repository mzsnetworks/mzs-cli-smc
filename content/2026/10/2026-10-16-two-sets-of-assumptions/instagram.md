# Instagram — two-sets-of-assumptions


An acquisition doesn't join two networks. 🤝 It joins two sets of assumptions about who is trusted.

And nobody documented either one.

→ Overlapping address space — solvable
→ Routing between two cores — solvable
→ DNS resolving both ways — solvable

⚠️ The half that overruns is the part nobody wrote down.

A flat segment carrying production because ten years ago everyone was in one building. A rule permitting a range for a project that ended in 2021.

Neither is written as a policy. Both are policies.

Connect them and you don't get the union of two security postures. You get the weaker one, applied to everything. 🔓

Which assumptions would you be inheriting?
