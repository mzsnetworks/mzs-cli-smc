# Facebook — first-hour-of-an-outage


The most expensive hour of an outage is the first one.

It's rarely spent diagnosing. It's spent deciding whose problem this is.

The bridge fills up. Each team demonstrates in turn that the fault isn't theirs. Network: clean interfaces. App team: responding on localhost. Storage: green. Every demonstration is honest, competent, and takes twelve minutes.

At the end of the hour everybody is correct and nothing is fixed.

That isn't defensiveness. Nobody can assign the problem, so everybody defends against being assigned it.

Separate ownership from cause. Ownership is a role, assigned at minute zero. Cause is a finding, for the postmortem.

Who is allowed to assign ownership on your bridge?
