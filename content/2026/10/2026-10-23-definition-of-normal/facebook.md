# Facebook — definition-of-normal


Anomaly detection needs a definition of normal, and most networks have never had a quiet enough week to learn one.

That's the part the demo skips. Clean baseline, injected fault, everyone impressed. Then it goes into an environment that's been running slightly wrong for three years.

The interface that resets every few days becomes normal. The link that saturates every afternoon becomes normal. None of it was labeled as broken.

So you get a system that's quietest about your oldest problems — because by the only definition it has, the chronic fault isn't an anomaly. It's the baseline.
