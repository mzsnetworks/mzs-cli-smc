# Facebook — time-to-safe-change


Automation coverage is the wrong metric.

Every program reports it because it's the one you can compute. The number climbs every quarter and nobody can say whether anything got better.

Coverage measures how much you built. It says nothing about whether change got safer or faster.

The metric that holds up is time-to-safe-change: from "we've decided" to "it's live, verified, and revertible" — including the waiting.

If the script runs in four seconds but the advisory board meets on Thursdays, your number is a week. That's the truth about your operation, whatever the dashboard says.

Which one is on your dashboard?
